PHOTO SPECIFICATIONS

Helpful Tips Before Your Visit

We take care of all dimensional cropping and lighting on-site, but keeping these quick points in mind ensures the fastest turnaround.

TIP 01 · CONTRAST

Clothing Contrast

Official UAE and visa photos require a plain white background. Wearing darker attire (navy, black, charcoal) ensures sharp outline contrast.

ATTIRE GUIDELINE ✓
TIP 02 · EXPRESSION

Neutral Expression

Government biometric scanners require eyes open, mouth closed, and a straight, centered gaze without shadows on facial features.

ICA STANDARD ✓
TIP 03 · HEADWEAR

Headwear & Glasses

Religious headwear (such as Hijabs or Turbans) is fully compliant provided the face is visible from chin to forehead without glare.
